3.6Cooking & Managing Moisture

Page 6 of 10 in Section 3
Orient

Orientation

Now the rice goes in. Your job on this page is to manage moisture and heat while it cooks.

Why it matters: Most problems with Jollof — dry, wet, undercooked or burnt rice — start here.

Learn

Add the rice and check the liquid

  1. Add the rice

    Add the prepared rice to the tomato and broth mixture and spread it so it is combined with the liquid and base.

  2. Check the liquid that’s already there

    Before adding water, look at how much liquid is in the pot. The tomato mixture and broth already provide moisture. Softer rice may need less extra water.

  3. Add water only if needed

    If the liquid looks insufficient, add a small amount gradually. Never add a large amount at once — too much water makes the rice wet or mushy, and it is easier to fix too little liquid than too much.

Decision point: is there enough liquid?
YesDon’t add water. Continue cooking and keep watching.
NoAdd a small amount of water, then check again.
Learn

Cover, cook and monitor

  1. Cover the pot

    Put the lid on securely and cook over approximately medium heat. Avoid unnecessarily high heat.

  2. Let it cook — but don’t walk away

    Don’t keep stirring: frequent stirring can interfere with cooking and the grain texture. But check it regularly.

  3. Monitor

    Each time you check, look at whether:

    • the rice is absorbing the liquid
    • there is enough moisture for the grains to cook
    • the grains are softening and cooking evenly
    • the bottom is starting to burn
    • there is excess liquid
Why medium heat?

If the heat is too high, the bottom of the pot gets too hot. The lower rice can burn while the upper rice is still undercooked. Medium heat helps it cook through evenly.

Watch / Observe

Recognize the moisture level

Compare the three pots and notice:
  • how the grains clump when the rice is too dry
  • how separate and fluffy they look when it is right
  • the liquid pooling between swollen grains when it is too wet
Pot 1Too dryStiff, clumped grains.
Pot 2 · TargetJust rightFully cooked, soft and generally separate.
Pot 3Too wetExcess liquid and swollen, mushy grains.

What do you do when you see pot 1 or pot 3? That is exactly what the next page, 3.7 Troubleshooting, covers.

Self-check

Quick check: moisture

You’ve added the rice. The tomato mixture and broth already seem to cover it well. What next?
Remember

Key takeaways

  • Check the existing liquid before you add any water.
  • Add water only in small amounts, only when needed.
  • Cover, cook on about medium heat, stir rarely — but check regularly.
