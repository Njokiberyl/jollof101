Accessibility

Jollof 101 is designed so that everyone can use it, following Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles and WCAG 2.1 AA guidance.

What we built in

  • Multiple representations: short text supported by labelled images, a map, comparison tables, and a captioned demonstration video with a full text alternative.
  • Keyboard access: every link, button, question and checklist works with the keyboard alone (Tab, Shift + Tab, Space and Enter). A “Skip to main content” link appears first on every page.
  • Meaning is never shown by colour alone: feedback uses words and symbols (“✓ Correct”, “✗ Not quite”), progress is stated in text (“Page 3 of 6”), and viewed pages are labelled “✓ Viewed”.
  • Readable text: a large base font size, short paragraphs, strong colour contrast and a logical heading order.
  • Alternative text for every meaningful image. Important instructions are written in the page, not inside images.
  • Video: captions can be turned on in the player (CC button), and a text alternative to the demonstration describes each stage and the cues to watch for.
  • Mobile friendly: pages adapt to phone, tablet and desktop screens.
  • Printable: the final preparation checklist prints cleanly for use in the kitchen.
  • Alternative evidence: if you cannot submit photos of your cooking, an equivalent non-photo option is available — ask your instructor.

Report a barrier

If anything in this module is hard to use, please tell us through the Contact page. Describe the page and what happened, and we will provide an alternative.
