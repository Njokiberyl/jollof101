Orient

Orientation

You are ready to apply everything you have learned. Prepare Nigerian Jollof rice using the recipe provided and the cooking sequence in this module.

Why it matters: This is the performance the whole module has been building toward.

Learning goal — what you will show

“Given the selected Nigerian Jollof rice recipe, required ingredients, and standard home-kitchen equipment, the learner will prepare Jollof rice by completing all critical preparation and cooking steps in the appropriate sequence and producing a finished dish that meets the established food-safety, doneness, texture, appearance, and flavor criteria.”

Learn

Your task

  1. Prepare the dish

    Follow the recipe and the module’s sequence: chicken and broth → tomato base → broth and seasoning → rice → cooking and monitoring → quality check.

  2. Observe and decide

    Successful cooking means following instructions and making decisions based on what you see, smell and taste. Use the troubleshooting actions from page 3.7 if you need them.

  3. Use the checklist as you go

    Tick each item below as you complete it. You can print the checklist for the kitchen.

  4. Submit your evidence

    Submit through your course’s assignment page: your completed process and safety checklist, photos of key stages and the finished dish, and your reflection (page 3.10). If you can’t submit photos, ask your instructor about the equivalent non-photo option.

⚠ Safety comes first, whatever your quiz score

Follow every critical safety step. Check the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C), keep raw chicken separate, and actively monitor the pot while it is on the heat — don’t leave cooking unattended.

Your preparation is evaluated with the module’s performance rubric, using the food-safety, doneness, texture, appearance and flavour criteria you studied on page 3.8.
