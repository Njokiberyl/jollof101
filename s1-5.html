1.5Try It: Classification Challenge

Page 5 of 6 in Section 1

TRY IT: Can you classify what you’ve learned?

This activity measures Objective 1. It is formative: you get feedback on every item, and you can change your answers and check again.

Target: 80% accuracy
Objective 1 — what this activity measures

Given examples of staple foods, ingredients, and traditional dishes presented in the module, the learner will classify the examples into the appropriate food categories and cultural or regional contexts with at least 80% accuracy.

Success criterion: at least 80% of the 19 items correct (at least 16 of 19).

Assessment · Classify

Classification Challenge

Directions: Use the menu beside each item to classify it. When you have answered every item in Parts A and B, choose Check my answers.

Not sure? Go back to the definitions: a staple is the regular foundation of meals; an ingredient adds flavour, texture or nutrition; a dish is a completed preparation.

Part A — Staple food, ingredient or dish?

Part B — Which place is this dish or food most associated with?

Use the associations presented in Section 1.

Remember

Key takeaways

  • Classify by function: foundation of the meal (staple), component (ingredient) or finished preparation (dish).
  • The same food can sit in different categories depending on its form — rice is a staple; Jollof rice is a dish.
  • Some dishes belong to one place; others are shared across several countries.
