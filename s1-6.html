Looking ahead

Up next: why Jollof rice?

Jollof rice brings together everything in Section 1: a common staple (rice), familiar ingredients (tomatoes, onions, peppers, oil and seasonings), cooking techniques that turn them into a dish, and a strong role in celebration and identity across many countries.

In Section 2 you will discover where it comes from and why it is prepared differently from place to place.
