Orientation

After you have prepared the dish, take a few minutes to reflect. Thinking back over your decisions helps turn one cooking session into a skill you keep.

Section 3 summary

  1. You gathered ingredients and equipment and identified your rice variety.
  2. You cooked chicken safely to 165°F (74°C) and kept the broth.
  3. You prepared the tomato base and judged it by taste, then added broth and adjusted seasoning gradually.
  4. You prepared the rice for its variety and checked the liquid before adding water.
  5. You monitored heat and moisture, and learned what to do when rice is too dry, too wet, undercooked or burning.
Most importantly

Preparing Jollof rice takes observation and decision-making at every stage. Follow the recipe, watch for the cooking cues, adjust gradually and evaluate the finished rice before serving.

Remember

Key takeaways

  • Reflection turns one cooking session into lasting skill.
  • Cues — not timers — tell you when each stage is done.
  • Small, considered adjustments beat big, blind ones.
