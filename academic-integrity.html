Academic Integrity

The activities in Jollof 101 are designed to help you learn, so they only work when the work is genuinely yours.

  • Do your own thinking. Write your comparison (page 2.6) and reflection (page 3.10) in your own words before you look at the model response.
  • Report your cooking honestly. Tick checklist items only for steps you actually completed, and submit photos or evidence of your own preparation.
  • Credit your sources. If you draw on other recipes, videos or articles, name them. The module’s own sources are listed on the Wrap-Up page.
  • Self-checks are for practice. Retrying them is encouraged — that is how they help you learn.
  • Collaborate openly. Optional peer discussion is welcome in Section 2, but each comparison you submit should be your own.

Follow your institution’s academic integrity policy. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask your instructor first.
