3.4Broth & Seasoning

Page 4 of 10 in Section 3
Orient

Orientation

With a well-cooked tomato base, you now add the chicken broth you kept and adjust the seasoning.

Why it matters: The broth is already seasoned. Adding more without tasting first can make the dish too salty or too spicy.

Learn

Add, taste, adjust

  1. Add the broth

    Carefully add the reserved chicken broth to the tomato base. Stir and let them cook together so the flavours spread.

  2. Taste before seasoning

    Before adding anything, taste the mixture and ask:

    • Is there enough salt?
    • Is the pepper level right?
    • Can you taste the curry and thyme?
    • Is the overall seasoning balanced?

  3. Adjust gradually

    If it needs more, add a small amount, stir and taste again. Watch the pepper especially — it should add flavour and heat without overpowering everything else.

Decision point: does it need more seasoning?
BalancedDon’t add more. Move on.
Needs adjustmentAdd a little, stir, taste again. Repeat if needed.
Remember

It is easier to add a little more than to fix a mixture that has become too salty or spicy. Let the taste guide you — don’t season automatically.

Self-check

Quick check: seasoning

You have just added the chicken broth. The recipe lists salt and curry powder. What should you do first?
Remember

Key takeaways

  • The broth is already seasoned — always taste before adding more.
  • Adjust in small amounts and taste after each one.
  • Keep pepper in balance with the other flavours.
