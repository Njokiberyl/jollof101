Stage by stage

  1. Season and cook the chicken

    Seasonings are added to the chicken with water, and the chicken cooks until done. Cue: safety is confirmed with a food thermometer — 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part — not by time or colour. The seasoned broth is kept.

  2. Cook the onions

    Chopped onions cook in hot oil. Cue: they turn golden or slightly brown, without becoming too dark or burnt.

  3. Fry the tomato paste, then add the tomato mixture

    Tomato paste is stirred into the onions and fried in the oil, then the blended fresh tomato mixture goes in. Cue: stir so nothing sticks or burns.

  4. Cook the tomato base until it is ready

    The mixture reduces from thin and watery to thick and rich. Cue: taste — keep cooking while it still tastes sour.

  5. Add the broth and adjust seasoning

    The kept chicken broth is added and combined. Cue: taste before adding more seasoning, then adjust a little at a time.

  6. Prepare and add the rice

    Rice is prepared for its variety — basmati washed with hot water, harder rice parboiled for about five minutes — and added to the pot. Cue: check the liquid already in the pot before adding water.

  7. Cover and cook on medium heat

    The pot is covered and cooked over about medium heat, without frequent stirring but with regular checks. Cue: watch for absorption, softening grains, even cooking, excess liquid and any burning at the bottom.

  8. Manage moisture if needed

    If the rice is too wet, foil goes tightly over the pot with the lid on top, and cooking continues on about medium heat, checked after about five minutes. If it is too dry, a small amount of water is added.

  9. Check the finished rice

    Cue: grains fully cooked, comfortably soft and generally separate; even red to deep-orange colour; not wet or mushy; appealing aroma; balanced taste with no sourness.
